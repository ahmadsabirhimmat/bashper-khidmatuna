Privacy policy

This policy explains how Bashper Khidmatuna collects and uses information in the mobile app, provider portal, and admin console.

Last updated: 10 September 2026

Who we are

Bashper Khidmatuna is a public directory of emergency and essential service contacts in Kandahar, Afghanistan. It is operated independently and is not an official government dispatch system.

What we collect

Account details you provide: name, email address, phone number, and password. If you use Sign in with Google, we receive your Google account name, email address, and a Google user ID so we can create or link your account. We do not receive your Google password.

Provider listings you submit: organization name, phone numbers, location, district, description, and photos.

Location permission on the mobile app is optional. If you allow it, the app uses your approximate location only while the app is open, to sort nearby services. We do not store a live location history on the server.

Favorites are stored on your device, not on our server.

How we use information

We use this data to create and secure your account, send email verification and password-reset codes, publish approved emergency contacts, and operate the Kandahar directory. We do not sell personal data.

Sharing

We share data only with services needed to run the app: hosting, email delivery, and Google Sign-In if you choose that button. Approved provider listings (name, phone, location, district, photo) are visible to people who use the directory.

Retention and deletion

You can delete your account in the app or provider website under Profile, or follow Delete your account. Deleting an account removes your user record, verification codes, and any provider numbers or photos you own. Email deletion requests sent to bashperkhidmatuna@gmail.com are completed within 30 days. Public emergency lines managed by administrators (such as 119 and 102) stay in the directory.

Age requirement

You must be at least 13 years old to create an account and use Bashper Khidmatuna.

Disclaimer

This app is a public directory. It does not replace national emergency numbers. In a life-threatening emergency, call 119 (police) or 102 (ambulance), or the nearest verified local service.

Contact

Questions about this policy: bashperkhidmatuna@gmail.com
